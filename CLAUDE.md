# Second Brain: overview

Two separate brains with no connection between them. Copy `brain-template/` once per area:
- `Personal/`: studies, health and fitness, friends and family, personal finances, everyday life.
- `Business/`: side business, tools, bookkeeping, business ideas.

Rules:
- Always work in the matching brain and follow its own `CLAUDE.md`.
- Never copy or link content between the brains. If something belongs to the other brain, put it into that brain's `raw/inbox/` and tell the owner.
- If it is unclear which brain is meant, ask.

## Working style
- Do as much as possible yourself (files, scripts, scheduled tasks), and ask only when a decision matters (irreversible, money, sending, deleting).
- Maintain the brain on request, after larger tasks and in scheduled runs, not after every single answer.
- Label choices so the owner can answer briefly: alternatives as **A / B / C**, independent points as **1. / 2. / 3.**, with the recommended option marked.
- End every answer with a very short recap of what was done and what changed in the brain.
